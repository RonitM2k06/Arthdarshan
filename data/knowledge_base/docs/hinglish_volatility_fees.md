Volatility ka matlab hai ki kisi cheez ka daam kitna aur kitni tezi se upar-neeche hota hai. Zyada utaar-chadhav matlab raaste mein zyada anishchitta. Ye hamesha asthayi nuksaan nahi hota, par badi halchal logon ko ghabrahat mein faisle karwa sakti hai. Koi bharosemand tareeke se nahi bata sakta ki daam kab aur kitne badlenge.

Fees wo kharcha hai jo aap kisi product ko rakhne ya use karne ke liye dete ho: entry charge, har saal ka charge (expense ratio), exit charge aur brokerage. Har saal ka charge aapke khilaf compound hota hai kyunki wo us balance se kata jaata hai jo badhna chahiye. Hypothetical udaharan: Rs 1,00,000 par 8% saalana growth 20 saal mein lagbhag Rs 4,66,096 hota hai; 2.5% saalana charge ke saath wahi lagbhag Rs 2,91,776 rehta hai. Hamesha saare charges rupees mein, likhit mein maango.
