No single sign proves fraud, but several together are a reason to stop and verify before paying anything.

Common warning signs: a guaranteed return, or a very high return with "no risk"; pressure to decide today or in minutes; "only a few slots left"; contact from a stranger who added you to a group; claims of registration or approval that you cannot check yourself; payment requests to a personal account or UPI ID instead of a company account; a fee that must be paid before you can withdraw your own money; refusal to give written details, a company name or a video call; testimonials and screenshots as the only proof; asking you to keep it secret; a new payment demanded after you paid the first one.

Scams escalate in steps. Each payment makes it feel as if stopping means losing what was already paid. The earliest checkpoint — before paying — costs nothing. Money already paid is gone either way; paying more does not bring it back.
