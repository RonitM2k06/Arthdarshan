Use this order: Claim, Source, Authority, Evidence, Independent verification, Decision.

Claim: write down exactly what is promised. Source: who is saying it, and did you ask them, or did they contact you? Authority: any claim of registration, approval or expertise is only a claim until you check it. Evidence: what independent proof exists beyond the seller's own material? Independent verification: check through a source the seller does not control. Type the regulator's official website address yourself; do not use links, numbers, screenshots or certificates sent by the person asking for money. Decision: decide only after the check. If you cannot verify it, treat it as unverified and do not pay.

A friend's recommendation, a crowded group chat, a polished design or a confident tone are not independent evidence. They can all be copied or faked. Missing an unverified offer costs nothing; acting on it can cost savings.
