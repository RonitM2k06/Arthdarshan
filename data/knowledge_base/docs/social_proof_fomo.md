Social proof is our habit of copying what many others seem to be doing. It is a shortcut, not evidence. Crowds can be wrong, paid or entirely fake: bot accounts, selected screenshots and testimonials are cheap to produce. When many people repeat the same claim, ask whether any of them checked it.

FOMO, the fear of missing out, is created with countdown timers, shrinking slot counters, and stories of friends who already gained. These tactics are designed to make you act before you verify. A useful habit is to name the pressure out loud and then wait ten minutes. If an offer cannot survive ten minutes of waiting, it cannot survive checking either.

A friend can be sincere and still be fooled. Someone paid a referral bonus has a reason to push you even if they are honest. A friend's recommendation is a reason to look closer, not a reason to skip checking.
