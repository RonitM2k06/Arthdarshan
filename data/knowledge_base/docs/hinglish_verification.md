Ye kram apnao: Daava, Source, Authority, Saboot, Swatantra verification, Faisla.

Daava: likho ki theek-theek kya vaada hai. Source: kaun keh raha hai, aapne pucha ya unhone aapse sampark kiya? Authority: registration ya approval ka daava tab tak sirf daava hai jab tak aap khud check na karo. Saboot: seller ke apne material ke alawa swatantra saboot kya hai? Swatantra verification: aise source se check karo jise seller control nahi karta. Regulator ki official website ka address khud type karo; paise maangne wale ke bheje link, number, screenshot ya certificate use mat karo. Faisla: jaanch ke baad karo. Verify na ho paye to use unverified maanke paise mat do.

Dost ki salah, bheed wala group chat, sundar design ya aatmavishwas bhari awaaz swatantra saboot nahi hain; ye sab copy ya nakli banaye ja sakte hain.
