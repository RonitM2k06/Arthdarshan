Koi ek sanket fraud sabit nahi karta, par kai sanket saath hon to paise dene se pehle ruk kar verify karo.

Aam sanket: guaranteed return ya bahut ooncha return "no risk" ke saath; aaj hi ya kuch minutes mein faisla karne ka dabaav; "sirf kuch slots bache hain"; anjaan ne group mein add karke offer bheja; registration ka daava jo aap khud check nahi kar sakte; payment kisi vyakti ke personal account ya UPI ID mein; apna paisa nikalne se pehle fee; likhit jaankari, company ka naam ya video call se inkaar; sirf screenshots aur testimonials ka saboot; chup rehne ko kehna; pehli payment ke baad nayi payment ki maang.

Scam kadam-dar-kadam badhte hain. Har payment ke baad lagta hai ruka to diya hua paisa doob jayega. Sabse pehla checkpoint paise dene se pehle hai aur usmein kuch kharch nahi hota. Jo paisa de diya wo dono taraf gaya hi hai; aur dene se wapas nahi aata.
