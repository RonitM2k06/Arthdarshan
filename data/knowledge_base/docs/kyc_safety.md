KYC (Know Your Customer) is identity and address verification that regulated financial institutions do before opening or maintaining an account. It should be done through the institution's official app, website or branch that you opened yourself.

A common scam pattern: a call or message says your KYC has expired and asks you to click a link or read out a code you just received, or your account will be blocked today. Genuine institutions do not ask you to read out one-time codes, PINs or passwords. If contacted, hang up and call the institution using the number on its official website or on your card.

Never share one-time codes, PINs, passwords, card numbers or similar details with anyone who contacts you. ARTHDARSHAN never asks for them either.
