Stop paying. Do not send "one last fee" to release or recover money. Keep screenshots, chats, payment receipts and account details as evidence. Tell a trusted person; shame keeps scams alive and people of every age are targeted.

In India you can report financial cyber fraud on the national cyber-crime helpline 1930 and on the national cyber-crime reporting portal (cybercrime.gov.in); check those official sources for current details. Contact your bank immediately through the number on its official website or card so it can try to act on the payment.

Beware of "recovery agents" who message you offering to get money back for a fee; that is a second scam. Official complaint routes do not charge you to ask for an explanation or to file a complaint.
