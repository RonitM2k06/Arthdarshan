Volatility describes how much and how fast a price moves up and down. A fund that moves between 90 and 110 is less volatile than one that moves between 60 and 140. Volatility is not the same as permanent loss, but big swings can push people to react emotionally.

Nobody can reliably predict when prices will move or by how much. Fear and greed both create urgent orders: "sell now before it goes to zero" and "buy now, it is a discount" are the same pressure in opposite directions, and neither knows the future. A person who was right once may simply have been lucky; hindsight makes outcomes look obvious.

Preparing in advance helps. When calm, write down your goal, your time horizon and how large a fall you can handle without panic. Use that plan to check headlines instead of letting headlines decide.
